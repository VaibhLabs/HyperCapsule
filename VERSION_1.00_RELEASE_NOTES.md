## 🌟 Welcome to HyperCapsule 1.00 Stable

HyperCapsule reaches its first official stable release! Crafted by **VaibhLabs**, this release brings a complete human-art-directed visual refinement, fluid tactile animations, rock-solid layout stability, and deeply customizable dynamic notification islands for Android and Xiaomi HyperOS.

---

## 🚀 What's New in 1.00 Stable

### 🎨 Human-Crafted Aesthetic & Visual Hierarchy
- **AMOLED Glass Architecture:** Refined deep black canvas (`#06080C`) with subtle layered spatial elevation, eliminating harsh generic neon glows.
- **Precision Specular Borders:** 1dp subtle specular highlights replace blinding gradient outlines for a mature, commercial-grade look.
- **Standardized Typography Scale:** Clear typographic rhythm with high-contrast text and comfortable touch targets compliant with WCAG 2.1 AA standards.

### 🏝️ Re-Engineered Island Behavior
- **Live Dynamic Island Simulator:** Real-time visual preview displaying ambient notification pills, quick reply badges, and auto-close timers.
- **Organized Functional Modules:**
  - *Display & Timeout:* Master auto-hide toggle with step-snapped duration slider (1s to 1h).
  - *Floating Popup Presentation:* Separate heads-up duration control and lockscreen/shade presentation toggles.
  - *Interception & Quick Actions:* Suppress system notifications with clear warning alerts, enable synchronized dismissal, and activate inline quick replies.

### 🛠️ Glitch-Free Design Tab
- **Fixed Side Black Bar:** Replaced unstable experimental carousels with bounded, edge-to-edge aware pagers and lazy rows.
- **Fluid 120Hz Scrolling:** Smooth horizontal scrolling with zero clip escapes or gesture conflicts.

### 🎬 Tactile Physics & Spring Animations
- **Physical Spring Switches:** Switches bounce into place with organic spring damping and dynamic thumb morphing.
- **Accordion Disclosures:** Dependent settings glide open with spring expansion.
- **Tactile Micro-Press:** Responsive card compression feedback on touch with subtle haptic ticks.

### 🎭 Theme Studio Mini Island Previews
- Theme cards now feature realistic mini dynamic island pills displaying exact highlight colors, icon shapes, and pill contours.
- Full preservation of open `.hbr` theme package formats and schema compatibility.

### 🏢 Developer Identity & Community
- **VaibhLabs Official Branding:** Clear developer attribution throughout the application.
- **Official Links:** Developer action button links directly to official repository at `https://github.com/VaibhLabs/HyperCapsule`.
- **Streamlined Settings:** Cleaned up redundant Version History and Special Thanks entries while keeping open-source license compliance intact.
