HyperCapsule Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

100% Offline & Private

Core Philosophy

HyperCapsule does NOT collect, store, or transmit your personal data. The app does not request or hold the Android Internet access permission (android.permission.INTERNET). All notification alerts, app preferences, and customizations remain strictly and exclusively on your local device.

1. Permissions & Data Handling

  • Notification Access (NotificationListenerService): Used exclusively to detect active notifications (messages, phone calls, navigation steps, media playback, and timers) and render them in the dynamic capsule overlay. Notification content is processed in volatile memory (RAM) and is cleared immediately upon dismissal. No notification text is written to storage or logged.
  • Package Visibility (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Allows you to discover installed applications on your device, choose which apps appear in the capsule, and configure per-app rules. App packages and names are cached locally on-device.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used solely to display the floating inline quick reply interface and interactive overlay windows without leaving your current app. Reply text is forwarded directly to the source app via Android's RemoteInput framework.
  • Optional Shizuku Integration: An advanced local feature for Xiaomi Chinese HyperOS ROMs to toggle system focus notification parameters locally without any external communication.
  • Boot Completed & Wake Lock: Resumes background listener services cleanly following device restart.

2. Storage & Backup

All application settings, toggle states, custom keyword blocklists, and widget configurations reside exclusively in Android's sandboxed private app storage using Room SQLite database and DataStore preferences. Manual backup export files (.hbr) are created only upon your explicit request and saved to a destination you select.

3. Zero Third-Party Telemetry

HyperCapsule contains no advertising networks, no analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), and no remote crash reporting SDKs. All operations are 100% local and offline.
